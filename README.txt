ELITE EDUCATION — B.TECH CREDIT TRANSFER HOSTING PACKAGE

FILES
- index.html : complete landing page
- assets/elite-logo.jpeg : supplied Elite Education logo

UPLOAD
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Upload the contents of this folder to your hosting public_html (or the target campaign folder).
3. Open index.html / your campaign URL.

BEFORE RUNNING ADS
1. In index.html, replace YOUR_WHATSAPP_NUMBER with the actual WhatsApp number in international digits-only format.
2. Replace YOUR_PIXEL_ID with the Meta Pixel ID.
3. Replace the form submit placeholder with your CRM/webhook endpoint.
4. Add privacy policy / consent links required by your organisation and ad platform.
5. Verify every university, affiliation, approval, recognition, placement, job, WES/MEA/AICTE/UGC or similar claim for the exact programme before publishing. The supplied source document contains such references, but this landing page intentionally avoids presenting them as blanket verified claims.
6. Confirm the exact programme availability and university approval rules for each lead before making an admission promise.

SOURCE BASIS
The page follows the supplied 7-page B.Tech Credit Transfer document: student situations, why credit transfer may be considered, academic evaluation process, limitations, listed B.Tech programmes, FAQ themes, and five application steps.

CAMPAIGN POSITIONING
Problem -> Eligibility check -> Academic details -> Evaluation -> University approval -> Admission confirmation.

The page is intentionally written in simple English for Indian paid-traffic audiences and avoids guaranteed transfer claims.
