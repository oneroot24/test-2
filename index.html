Simple, transparent approach

Your previous study deserves an academic review.

Credit transfer is about evaluating completed subjects and credits against the requirements of a new programme. It is not an automatic transfer promise.

  • Submit marksheets, transcripts and relevant documents
  • Completed subjects and credits are evaluated
  • Eligibility is checked against university rules
  • Eligible credits may be transferred if approved
Why explore credit transfer?

Continue without starting the conversation from zero.

The supplied material highlights avoiding repetition of eligible completed subjects, continuing interrupted education, using previously earned credits where permitted, and saving time and academic effort.

Important

Not every previous credit will necessarily be accepted. Course/syllabus matching may be required, some semesters or subjects may need to be repeated, and recognition can vary by use case. Final decisions depend on the university and applicable approval rules.

FAQ

Questions students usually ask.

Is B.Tech credit transfer possible?

It may be possible, but the supplied material states that it depends on the university, previous credits and eligibility or approval rules. A case should be evaluated rather than treated as an automatic transfer.

Will all my previous credits be accepted?

No guarantee should be given. The supplied brief notes that not all previous credits may be accepted and that course or syllabus matching may be required.

Could I have to repeat a subject or semester?

Yes. The supplied material notes that some semesters or subjects may need to be repeated depending on the evaluation.

What documents should I submit?

The process in the supplied brief starts with previous B.Tech marksheets, transcripts and relevant academic documents so completed subjects and credits can be evaluated.

Which university will approve my transfer?

University approval is part of the process. The appropriate option depends on your eligibility, academic background and the applicable university's rules.

What should I do first?

Start with the eligibility check on this page. Share your branch, study status, semesters completed and previous university so your case can be understood before discussing next steps.
